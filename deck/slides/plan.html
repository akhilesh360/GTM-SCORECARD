90-day plan

From proof of concept to the portfolio standard

Days 1–30 · Connect
Load real exports for both companies. Add their source aliases. Stand up HubSpot properties and the agent webhook. Baseline the scorecard.
Days 31–60 · Act
Shift Acme outbound budget toward referral and call tracking. Fix Summit's missed calls with a call-back SLA. Turn on DeepSeek scoring and compare it to the rules score.
Days 61–90 · Scale
Onboard the next portfolio company from config alone. Review the scorecard monthly with operators. Hand the playbook to a GTM engineer to run.
Success at day 90: every company on the same definitions, the agent handling every new lead, and one dashboard the board reads monthly.
